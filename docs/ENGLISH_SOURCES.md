# English course source methodology

The English-for-Spanish-speakers path is newly authored for OSAPHLA. The books in
the adjacent source library informed its scope, progression, communicative domains,
pronunciation coverage, and assessment mix. No book text, illustrations, exercises,
audio, PDFs, or EPUBs are copied into this repository or the production bundle.

The principal references were:

- *Gramática De La Lengua Inglesa: A Complete English Grammar Workbook for Spanish Speakers* — sequencing and Spanish-language contrastive explanations;
- *Practice Makes Perfect Basic English* and *Easy English Step-by-Step for ESL Learners* — beginner grammar and controlled-practice progression;
- *Side by Side* 1–3 — communicative functions, spiraled review, life skills, and dialogue domains;
- *Pronunciation Pairs* — sound contrasts, stress, rhythm, linking, and intelligibility;
- *Barron's Visual Dictionary: English for Spanish Speakers* and the thematic English–Spanish dictionaries — concrete vocabulary domains;
- *English Travel Phrases for Spanish Speakers* and the traveler phrase collections — transport, lodging, food, directions, and problem solving;
- *Collins Get Ready for IELTS Listening* and *Speaking*, Cambridge IELTS practice books, and Collins IELTS key words — advanced listening, speaking, register, evidence, and test-task awareness;
- *The Blue Book of Grammar and Punctuation* and the intermediate ESL grammar reference — editing, punctuation, and later-course form review;
- the bilingual beginner stories — extensive-reading difficulty, reconstruction, and inference progression;
- *More Than a Native Speaker* — learner-centered classroom language and intercultural framing.

The 36-week synthesis applies those references in bands:

| Weeks | Course emphasis | Reference emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–7 | sound system, first contact, `be`, articles, present tense, questions, and repair | contrastive grammar, beginner workbooks, and pronunciation pairs |
| 8–16 | family, routines, food, shopping, city services, travel, health, and weather | visual vocabulary, *Side by Side*, and travel/life-skills collections |
| 17–24 | past narration, objects, reference, perfects, and procedures | step-by-step and intermediate grammar, stories, and controlled reconstruction |
| 25–32 | future, conditionals, modality, clauses, passive voice, idiom, work, and technology | intermediate grammar, punctuation/editing, life skills, and IELTS preparation |
| 33–36 | sources, global English, professional listening, argument, and capstone performance | IELTS listening/speaking/writing, intercultural teaching, and advanced key words |

The curriculum uses General American English as its production model. It teaches
learners to understand common international variation without asking them to imitate
an identity or accent. The reference library remains outside this Git repository;
`.gitignore` also excludes PDF and EPUB files as a safeguard.
