Form and meaning. Quantities; count and mass nouns; direct objects; polite requests with querer, poder, and quisiera.

Model set. Quisiera probar el plato del día. ¿Cuánto cuesta un kilo de tomates? Para mí, una sopa sin carne. El camarero nos trae la cuenta. Disculpe, pedí agua y no refresco. English meaning: I would like to try the daily special. How much does a kilogram of tomatoes cost? For me, a soup without meat. The waiter brings us the bill. Excuse me, I ordered water, not soda.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: buy food; order a meal; handle dietary needs and problems. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
