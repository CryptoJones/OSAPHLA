Operational objective. By the end of this section you can define a requirement, identify known and unknown people or things, connect complex information. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Relative pronouns; indicative versus subjunctive in adjective clauses; lo que, quien, el que, and cuyo.

Sound and delivery. Long sentence phrasing with audible relative-clause boundaries.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
