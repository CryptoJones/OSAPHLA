Operational objective. By the end of this section you can give a safe procedure, offer advice, distinguish request from order. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Tú, usted, ustedes, and nosotros commands; pronoun placement; negative commands; deber and conviene.

Sound and delivery. Command prominence softened by por favor, intonation, and conditional phrasing.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
