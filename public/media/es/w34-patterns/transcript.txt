Form and meaning. Applying the form. Tú/vos/usted/vosotros are regional address systems, not errors to correct — recognize each verb form (tienes/tenés/tiene/tenéis) without judging one as more correct.

Model set. Tú tienes razón. Vos tenés razón. Usted tiene razón. Vosotros tenéis razón. Cada sistema de tratamiento es válido. You are right. You are right. You are right. You all are right. Each address system is valid.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: recognize major regional forms; ask for clarification without judgment; adapt address and vocabulary. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
