Form and meaning. Applying the form. Accidental se constructions use se + indirect pronoun + verb + subject to show something happened unintentionally: se me rompió el vaso.

Model set. Se me rompió el vaso. Se te olvidó la cita. Se le perdieron las llaves. Se nos cayó el papel. No fue mi culpa; se rompió solo. I broke the glass (accidentally). You forgot the appointment. He or she lost the keys. We dropped the paper. It wasn't my fault; it broke on its own.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: report accidental events; give concise instructions; maintain reference across sentences. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
