Operational objective. By the end of this section you can summarize a meeting, write a professional message, explain a technical problem and next action. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Reported speech; reciprocal constructions; stressed possessives; formal connectors and nominal style.

Sound and delivery. Chunk information-dense speech and articulate acronyms, numbers, and borrowed terms.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
