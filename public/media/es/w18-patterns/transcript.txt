Form and meaning. Irregular preterite stems; ser/ir; dar/ver; conocer, poder, querer, and saber in completed contexts.

Model set. No pude entrar porque perdí la llave. Fuimos al pueblo por otra carretera. Allí conocimos al nuevo alcalde. Supe la verdad esa misma tarde. Quiso ayudar, pero no tuvo tiempo. I couldn't get in because I lost the key. We went to the town by another road. There we met the new mayor. I learned the truth that same afternoon. He or she wanted to help but did not have time.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: report discoveries and attempts; distinguish intention from outcome; summarize a trip. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
