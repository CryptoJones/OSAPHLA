Operational objective. By the end of this section you can describe habits, ask about routines, say how often something happens. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Regular -ar, -er, and -ir present tense; subject omission; frequency expressions.

Sound and delivery. Keep verb endings audible; do not reduce unstressed syllables to schwa.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
