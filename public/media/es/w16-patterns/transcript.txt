Form and meaning. Weather expressions; present progressive; impersonal se; conditional planning with si + present.

Model set. Mañana va a llover en la costa. Hace viento en las zonas altas. Si cambia el tiempo, regresamos temprano. Se prohíbe encender fuego en el bosque. Estamos protegiendo una fuente de agua. It is going to rain on the coast tomorrow. It is windy in the highlands. If the weather changes, we will return early. Lighting fires in the forest is prohibited. We are protecting a water source.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: understand a forecast; describe terrain; change plans because of conditions. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
