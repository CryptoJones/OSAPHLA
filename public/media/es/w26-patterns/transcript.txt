Form and meaning. Simple conditional; irregular stems; future-in-the-past; conditional courtesy; si + imperfect subjunctive preview.

Model set. Yo elegiría la segunda opción. ¿Podría enviarme los detalles? El cambio reduciría el riesgo. Dijo que llegaría al día siguiente. En cambio, ese plan costaría demasiado. I would choose the second option. Could you send me the details? The change would reduce the risk. He or she said that they would arrive the following day. On the other hand, that plan would cost too much.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: make a tactful request; evaluate options; state hypothetical consequences. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
