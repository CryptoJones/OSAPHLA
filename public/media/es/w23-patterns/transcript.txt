Form and meaning. Applying the form. Present perfect combines haber (he/has/ha/hemos/han) with a past participle to link a past action to the present: he trabajado, ha llegado.

Model set. He trabajado aquí dos años. Has vivido en varios países. Ha llegado tarde hoy. Hemos terminado el proyecto. Han cambiado las reglas. I have worked here for two years. You have lived in several countries. He/she has arrived late today. We have finished the project. They have changed the rules.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: report experience and recent developments; connect past action to present relevance; sequence prior events. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
