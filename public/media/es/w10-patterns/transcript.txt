Form and meaning. Applying the form. Gustar-type verbs agree with the thing liked, not the person; use an indirect-object pronoun (me, te, le, nos, les) before the verb.

Model set. Me gusta el cine. Te gusta la música. Le interesa el arte. Nos encanta viajar. Les gusta el deporte. I like the cinema. You like music. Art interests him/her. We love traveling. They like sports.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: express likes and dislikes; invite and suggest; compare activities. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
