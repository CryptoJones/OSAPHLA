Form and meaning. Gustar-type verbs; indirect-object pronouns; infinitives after preference verbs; comparisons.

Model set. Me gusta escuchar música en vivo. A Tomás le interesan los documentales. ¿Prefieres salir o quedarte en casa? Nos encanta jugar juntos. El senderismo es más tranquilo que el fútbol. English meaning: I like listening to live music. Tomás is interested in documentaries. Do you prefer going out or staying home? We love playing together. Hiking is calmer than soccer.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: express likes and dislikes; invite and suggest; compare activities. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
