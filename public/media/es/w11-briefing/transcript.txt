Operational objective. By the end of this section you can buy food, order a meal, handle dietary needs and problems. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Quantities; count and mass nouns; direct objects; polite requests with querer, poder, and quisiera.

Sound and delivery. Single tap r and trill rr in high-frequency food and service vocabulary.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
