Form and meaning. Present subjunctive formation; noun clauses after will, influence, recommendation, necessity, and emotion.

Model set. Recomiendo que revise los datos. Es necesario que participen los vecinos. Me alegra que estés aquí. El director pide que lleguemos temprano. Ojalá encuentren una solución justa. I recommend that you review the data. It is necessary for the residents to participate. I am glad you are here. The director asks us to arrive early. I hope they find a fair solution.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: recommend and request action; express reaction; state necessity without overclaiming certainty. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
