Operational objective. By the end of this section you can ask for and give directions, identify public services, confirm a route. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Ir a; location prepositions; informal commands for directions; seguir and doblar.

Sound and delivery. Distinguish but accept seseo and distinción; practice place names respectfully.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
