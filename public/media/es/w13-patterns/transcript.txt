Form and meaning. Applying the form. Formal (usted) commands drop the -ar/-er/-ir ending and add the opposite-vowel ending: siga, doble, cruce, camine, vaya.

Model set. Siga derecho hasta la plaza. Doble a la izquierda en la esquina. Cruce la calle con cuidado. Camine dos cuadras más. Vaya hacia el norte. Go straight to the square. Turn left at the corner. Cross the street carefully. Walk two more blocks. Go north.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: ask for and give directions; identify public services; confirm a route. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
