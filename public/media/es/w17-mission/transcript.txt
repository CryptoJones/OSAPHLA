Mission brief. Deliver a concise incident report with who, what, when, where, and outcome.

Preparation. Select ten useful words, three linking expressions, and two repair phrases. Plan points rather than a memorized paragraph.

Performance. Complete the task once for fluency, review the evidence, then repeat it for greater clarity and accuracy.

After-action review. Record what succeeded, what blocked communication, and the single change that will matter most on the next attempt.
