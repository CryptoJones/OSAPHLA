Form and meaning. Applying the form. Some verbs use an irregular future stem (haré, tendré, podré) instead of the infinitive; the future tense can also express present probability: estará en casa (he's probably home).

Model set. Haré la tarea mañana. Tendré tiempo la próxima semana. Podré ayudarte más tarde. Estará en casa ahora. Tendrá unos treinta años. I will do the homework tomorrow. I will have time next week. I will be able to help you later. He/she is probably home now. He/she is probably about thirty.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: make forecasts; state commitments; express probability about the present. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
