Form and meaning. Question formation; interrogatives; ir, tener, hacer, venir, decir, poder, querer, and saber.

Model set. ¿Qué quiere hacer hoy? ¿Cuándo viene el autobús? No sé; ¿puede repetirlo? Tengo que hacer una llamada. ¿Por qué dices eso? What do you want to do today? When does the bus come? I don't know; can you repeat that? I have to make a call. Why do you say that?

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: request specific information; state ability and intention; repair a misunderstanding. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
