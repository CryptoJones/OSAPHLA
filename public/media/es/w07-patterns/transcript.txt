Form and meaning. Applying the form. Place the interrogative word first, then invert to verb-subject order; use saber for facts and conocer for people or places.

Model set. ¿Cuál es la mejor opción? ¿Cuánto cuesta esto? ¿Cuántos años tiene? No sé la respuesta. ¿Conoces este lugar? Which is the best option? How much does this cost? How old is he? I don't know the answer. Do you know this place?

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: request specific information; state ability and intention; repair a misunderstanding. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
