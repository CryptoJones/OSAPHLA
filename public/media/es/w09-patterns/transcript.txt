Form and meaning. Reflexive pronouns; routines; antes de/después de; present progressive.

Model set. Me levanto a las seis y media. Después de vestirme, preparo el desayuno. Ahora estoy esperando el tren. Los niños se acuestan temprano. ¿A qué hora te despiertas? I get up at six thirty. After getting dressed, I prepare breakfast. I am waiting for the train now. The children go to bed early. What time do you wake up?

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: sequence a routine; say what is happening now; compare schedules. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
