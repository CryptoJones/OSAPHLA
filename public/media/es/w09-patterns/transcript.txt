Form and meaning. Applying the form. Reflexive verbs need a pronoun that matches the subject (me, te, se, nos, se) placed right before the conjugated verb.

Model set. Me despierto y luego me levanto. Te levantas antes que yo. Se ducha por la mañana. Nos vestimos rápido los lunes. Por último, se acuestan a las diez. I wake up and then I get up. You get up before me. He/she showers in the morning. We get dressed quickly on Mondays. Lastly, they go to bed at ten.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: sequence a routine; say what is happening now; compare schedules. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
