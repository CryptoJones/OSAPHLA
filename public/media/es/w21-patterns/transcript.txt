Form and meaning. Applying the form. Object pronouns attach before a conjugated verb (lo envié) or after an infinitive/gerund/command (enviarlo); when le/les meets lo/la/los/las, le/les becomes se.

Model set. Lo envié ayer. La recibí esta mañana. Le mandé el mensaje. Se lo expliqué con calma. Me lo mostraron primero. I sent it yesterday. I received it this morning. I sent him/her the message. I explained it to him/her calmly. They showed it to me first.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: track who receives what; avoid unnecessary repetition; clarify a transaction. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
