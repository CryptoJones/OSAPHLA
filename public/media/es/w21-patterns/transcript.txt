Form and meaning. Direct- and indirect-object pronouns; placement with conjugated verbs, infinitives, gerunds, and commands.

Model set. Le envié el mensaje esta mañana. El paquete lo recibió otra persona. ¿Puedes mostrármelo? No les expliqué el cambio. Devuélvale los documentos al gerente. I sent him or her the message this morning. Someone else received the package. Can you show it to me? I did not explain the change to them. Return the documents to the manager.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: track who receives what; avoid unnecessary repetition; clarify a transaction. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
