Form and meaning. Applying the form. Use quisiera or podría for polite requests, and place the direct object right after the verb: pido el plato.

Model set. ¿Cuánto cuesta esto? Quisiera medio kilo de queso. ¿Me trae la cuenta, por favor? ¿Podría traerme una servilleta? Pido el plato sin cebolla. How much does this cost? I would like half a kilo of cheese. Could you bring me the bill, please? Could you bring me a napkin? I order the dish without onion.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: buy food; order a meal; handle dietary needs and problems. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
