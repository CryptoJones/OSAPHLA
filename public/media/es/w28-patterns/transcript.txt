Form and meaning. Subjunctive with doubt/denial; adverbial clauses; antes de que, para que, a menos que, and cuando with future reference.

Model set. Dudo que la cifra sea correcta. Es probable que llueva esta noche. Salimos temprano para que haya tiempo suficiente. No firmaremos a menos que cambien la cláusula. Llámame en cuanto llegues. I doubt that the figure is correct. It will probably rain tonight. We are leaving early so that there is enough time. We will not sign unless they change the clause. Call me as soon as you arrive.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: qualify certainty; state purpose and conditions; plan for contingencies. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
