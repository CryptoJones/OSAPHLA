Form and meaning. Applying the form. Use the subjunctive after expressions of doubt or denial, and after adverbial conjunctions like cuando, hasta que, and en cuanto when referring to a future, not-yet-realized event.

Model set. Dudo que llegue a tiempo. No niego que sea difícil. Cuando termine el proyecto, avísame. Espera hasta que confirmen. Tan pronto como sepa algo, te llamo. I doubt that he/she will arrive on time. I don't deny that it's difficult. When you finish the project, let me know. Wait until they confirm. As soon as I know something, I'll call you.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: qualify certainty; state purpose and conditions; plan for contingencies. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
