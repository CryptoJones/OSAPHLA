Form and meaning. Applying the form. Choose tú or usted based on formality and relationship; the verb ser changes with each subject pronoun.

Model set. Yo soy nueva aquí. ¿Tú eres de aquí? Usted es el director, ¿verdad? Nosotros somos compañeros de clase. Ella se llama Marta. I am new here. Are you from here? You are the director, right? We are classmates. Her name is Marta.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: greet and take leave; introduce yourself and another person; choose tú or usted. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
