Form and meaning. Subject pronouns, polite versus familiar address, present-tense ser for identity, and omitted subjects.

Model set. Hola, me llamo Alex. Mucho gusto, señora Rivera. ¿Cómo te llamas? Le presento a mi compañera. Nos vemos mañana; que tenga buen día. English meaning: Hello, my name is Alex. Nice to meet you, Mrs. Rivera. What is your name? Let me introduce you to my classmate. See you tomorrow; have a good day.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: greet and take leave; introduce yourself and another person; choose tú or usted. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
