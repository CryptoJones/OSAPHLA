Form and meaning. Applying the form. The imperfect describes ongoing or repeated past actions, age, time, weather, and background description — use era/tenía/había for state, and -aba/-ía endings for habitual action.

Model set. Yo tenía diez años. Tú vivías en otra ciudad. Él jugaba en la plaza. Nosotros éramos vecinos. Ellos hacían la tarea juntos. I was ten years old. You lived in another city. He played in the square. We were neighbors. They did homework together.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: describe how things used to be; set a scene; contrast past and present. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
