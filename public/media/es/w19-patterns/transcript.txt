Form and meaning. Imperfect forms; habitual past; age, time, weather, description, and ongoing background.

Model set. Antes había menos edificios. De niña jugaba en esta plaza. Eran las ocho y llovía mucho. Mientras trabajábamos, escuchábamos la radio. La ciudad parecía más tranquila. There used to be fewer buildings. As a girl, I used to play in this plaza. It was eight o'clock and raining heavily. While we worked, we listened to the radio. The city seemed quieter.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: describe how things used to be; set a scene; contrast past and present. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
