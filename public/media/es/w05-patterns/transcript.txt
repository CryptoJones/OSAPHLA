Form and meaning. Estar for location and condition; hay for existence; ser versus estar; basic prepositions.

Model set. La farmacia está cerca de la plaza. Hay un banco delante del hotel. El museo está cerrado hoy. ¿Dónde están los baños? La reunión es en la sala azul. English meaning: The pharmacy is near the plaza. There is a bank in front of the hotel. The museum is closed today. Where are the restrooms? The meeting is in the blue room.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: locate a place; describe a temporary condition; say what is available. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
