Form and meaning. Applying the form. Use doler with an indirect-object pronoun and the definite article for body parts (me duele la cabeza), and desde hace + time to say how long a symptom has lasted.

Model set. Me duele la pierna desde hace dos días. Me siento mal. Me duele el estómago. ¡Llame a un médico! Espere aquí, por favor. My leg has hurt for two days. I feel bad. My stomach hurts. Call a doctor! Wait here, please.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: describe symptoms; understand basic medical questions; request urgent help. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
