Form and meaning. Por versus para; passive voice; passive and impersonal se; agent suppression and focus.

Model set. La medida fue aprobada por el consejo. Se publicaron los resultados ayer. Trabajamos para mejorar el acceso. El servicio cerró por falta de fondos. Se requiere identificación para entrar. English meaning: The measure was approved by the council. The results were published yesterday. We work to improve access. The service closed because of a lack of funding. Identification is required to enter.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: explain purpose and cause; describe processes; report institutional action. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
