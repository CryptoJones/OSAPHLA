Form and meaning. Applying the form. Use había + participle for an action completed before another past event, and aunque + indicative for a known concession (aunque hay) vs. aunque + subjunctive for a hypothetical one.

Model set. El cambio ya había ocurrido antes del informe. Según se afirma, la reforma amplió derechos. Aunque hay consenso, el debate continúa. A pesar de que hay evidencia, algunos dudan. La consecuencia fue inmediata. The change had already occurred before the report. As is claimed, the reform expanded rights. Although there is consensus, the debate continues. Despite there being evidence, some doubt it. The consequence was immediate.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: summarize a historical development; distinguish fact from interpretation; compare perspectives. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
