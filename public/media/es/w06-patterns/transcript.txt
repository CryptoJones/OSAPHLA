Form and meaning. Regular -ar, -er, and -ir present tense; subject omission; frequency expressions.

Model set. Trabajo por la mañana y estudio por la noche. ¿Dónde vives? Comemos juntos los viernes. Ella escribe en su diario cada día. A veces bebo café después de comer. I work in the morning and study at night. Where do you live? We eat together on Fridays. She writes in her journal every day. Sometimes I drink coffee after eating.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: describe habits; ask about routines; say how often something happens. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
