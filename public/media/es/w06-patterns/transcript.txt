Form and meaning. Applying the form. Regular -ar/-er/-ir verbs take a different ending for each subject; Spanish often omits the subject pronoun because the verb ending already shows who acts.

Model set. Yo trabajo por la mañana. Tú estudias por la noche. Él vive en la ciudad. Nosotros comemos juntos los viernes. Ellos escriben todos los días. I work in the morning. You study at night. He lives in the city. We eat together on Fridays. They write every day.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: describe habits; ask about routines; say how often something happens. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
