Form and meaning. Applying the form. Match demonstratives to the noun's gender, number, and distance (este/esta/estos/estas near; ese/esa nearby; aquel/aquella far); use quedar for fit and costar for price.

Model set. Esta camisa me queda bien. Aquella chaqueta cuesta doscientos dólares. Estos zapatos son cómodos. Esas botas son caras. El vestido cuesta quinientos pesos. This shirt fits me well. That jacket over there costs two hundred dollars. These shoes are comfortable. Those boots are expensive. The dress costs five hundred pesos.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: ask for an item and size; compare price and fit; return or exchange a purchase. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
