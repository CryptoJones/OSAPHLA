Operational objective. By the end of this section you can summarize a historical development, distinguish fact from interpretation, compare perspectives. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Past perfect; reported evidence; concessive clauses; cause, consequence, and counterargument.

Sound and delivery. Paragraph-level intonation for claims, evidence, qualification, and conclusion.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
