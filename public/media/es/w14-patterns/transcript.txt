Form and meaning. Applying the form. Use acabar de + infinitive for something just finished, tener que + infinitive for obligation, and ir a + infinitive for near-future plans.

Model set. Acabo de llegar al aeropuerto. Tengo que confirmar mi vuelo. Voy a cambiar de tren. No quiero perder el autobús. El vuelo se retrasa una hora. I have just arrived at the airport. I have to confirm my flight. I am going to change trains. I don't want to miss the bus. The flight is delayed an hour.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: reserve transport and lodging; check in; report delays and room problems. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
