Form and meaning. Irregular present in travel; acabar de; tener que; sequencing future plans with ir a.

Model set. El tren sale del andén cuatro. Acabamos de llegar al aeropuerto. Tengo una reserva a nombre de Rivera. Mi equipaje no aparece. Vamos a quedarnos tres noches. English meaning: The train leaves from platform four. We have just arrived at the airport. I have a reservation under the name Rivera. My luggage is missing. We are going to stay three nights.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: reserve transport and lodging; check in; report delays and room problems. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
