Read for the situation. Cuando Teresa era niña, su calle tenía pocos autos y todos conocían a los vecinos. Cada tarde jugaba en la plaza mientras sus abuelos conversaban afuera. English meaning: When Teresa was a child, her street had few cars, and everyone knew the neighbors. Every afternoon she played in the plaza while her grandparents talked outside.

Read again for evidence. Identify who acts, what changes, when it happens, and which words establish the relationship between events.

Reading activities. Reading activity 45; Reading activity 46

Retell. Without looking back, give the main idea, three supporting details, and one reasonable inference in Spanish.
