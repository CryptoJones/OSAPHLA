Form and meaning. Applying the form. Regular preterite endings mark completed past action; verbs ending in -car, -gar, and -zar change spelling in the yo form (busqué, llegué, empecé) to keep the sound consistent.

Model set. Hablé con mi jefe ayer. Comí en un restaurante nuevo. Viví en esa ciudad dos años. Busqué la información toda la tarde. Llegué temprano la semana pasada. I spoke with my boss yesterday. I ate at a new restaurant. I lived in that city for two years. I searched for the information all afternoon. I arrived early last week.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: report what happened; sequence completed actions; ask follow-up questions. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
