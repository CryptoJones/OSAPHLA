Form and meaning. Regular preterite; spelling changes; time markers; completed event chains.

Model set. Ayer llegué temprano a la oficina. Primero revisamos el informe. Luego llamaron al supervisor. ¿Qué ocurrió después? La reunión terminó a las seis. Yesterday I arrived at the office early. First we reviewed the report. Then they called the supervisor. What happened afterward? The meeting ended at six.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: report what happened; sequence completed actions; ask follow-up questions. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
