Operational objective. By the end of this section you can request specific information, state ability and intention, repair a misunderstanding. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Question formation; interrogatives; ir, tener, hacer, venir, decir, poder, querer, and saber.

Sound and delivery. Question intonation and the difference between informational and confirmation questions.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
