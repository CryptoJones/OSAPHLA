Operational objective. By the end of this section you can ask for an item and size, compare price and fit, return or exchange a purchase. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Demonstratives; colors; numbers above one hundred; adjective placement; quedar and costar.

Sound and delivery. Voiced approximants b, d, and g between vowels.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
