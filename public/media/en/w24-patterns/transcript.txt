Forma y significado. Imperativos; should, must, have to y need to; secuencias; peticiones mitigadas y prohibiciones.

Conjunto de modelos. Turn off the equipment before opening it. Don't share that password. Make sure you save a copy. Let's check the data again. You should avoid this road at night.
