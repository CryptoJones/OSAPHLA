Operational objective. By the end of this section you can locate a place, describe a temporary condition, say what is available. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Estar for location and condition; hay for existence; ser versus estar; basic prepositions.

Sound and delivery. Spanish b/v share sounds; avoid the English v in neutral connected speech.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
