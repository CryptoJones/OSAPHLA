Operational objective. By the end of this section you can express likes and dislikes, invite and suggest, compare activities. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Gustar-type verbs; indirect-object pronouns; infinitives after preference verbs; comparisons.

Sound and delivery. Palatal ñ and y/ll variation without treating one regional realization as superior.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
