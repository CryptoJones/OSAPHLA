Operational objective. By the end of this section you can describe how things used to be, set a scene, contrast past and present. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Imperfect forms; habitual past; age, time, weather, description, and ongoing background.

Sound and delivery. Maintain the three-syllable rhythm of imperfect endings and distinguish -ía from -aba.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
