Form and meaning. Adjective agreement and position; possessive adjectives and pronouns; tener; personal a.

Model set. Mi hermana mayor es muy amable. Conozco a los padres de Elena. Nuestra familia vive en tres ciudades. ¿Es suyo este retrato? Tengo dos hermanos y una prima. English meaning: My older sister is very kind. I know Elena's parents. Our family lives in three cities. Is this portrait yours? I have two brothers and one female cousin.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: describe people and relationships; express possession; identify a person precisely. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.

Adjectives agree. A variable adjective matches the noun in gender and number: un amigo alto, una amiga alta, unos amigos altos, unas amigas altas. The noun controls the form of the adjective.

Not every adjective changes for gender. Adjectives ending in -e or many consonants commonly keep one singular form: un amigo amable and una amiga amable. They still change for plural number: amigos amables, amigas amables.

Position affects emphasis. Descriptive adjectives usually follow the noun. Some common adjectives can appear before it, sometimes with a change in emphasis or form. First secure agreement; then learn position as part of each expression.
