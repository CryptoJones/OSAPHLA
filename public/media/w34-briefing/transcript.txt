Operational objective. By the end of this section you can recognize major regional forms, ask for clarification without judgment, adapt address and vocabulary. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Tú, vos, usted, and vosotros systems; leísmo recognition; lexical variation; code-switching and register.

Sound and delivery. Seseo/distinción, yeísmo, aspiration of final s, voseo stress, and recognition without caricature.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
