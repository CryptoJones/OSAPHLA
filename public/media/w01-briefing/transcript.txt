Operational objective. By the end of this section you can spell a name, recognize a stressed syllable, build a sustainable study loop. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Spanish sound–spelling correspondence, the five stable vowels, syllables, stress, written accents, and punctuation.

Sound and delivery. Pure a/e/i/o/u vowels; silent h; strong and soft c and g; syllable-timed rhythm.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
