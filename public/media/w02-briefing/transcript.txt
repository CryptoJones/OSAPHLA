Operational objective. By the end of this section you can greet and take leave, introduce yourself and another person, choose tú or usted. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Subject pronouns, polite versus familiar address, present-tense ser for identity, and omitted subjects.

Sound and delivery. Link final vowels into the next word and keep unstressed vowels clear.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
