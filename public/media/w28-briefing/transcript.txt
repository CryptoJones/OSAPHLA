Operational objective. By the end of this section you can qualify certainty, state purpose and conditions, plan for contingencies. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Subjunctive with doubt/denial; adverbial clauses; antes de que, para que, a menos que, and cuando with future reference.

Sound and delivery. Clause grouping and contrastive emphasis on certainty markers.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
