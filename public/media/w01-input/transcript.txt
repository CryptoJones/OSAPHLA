Read for the situation. Ana abre su cuaderno. Escucha una palabra, marca la sílaba fuerte y la repite. No busca la perfección; busca una mejora clara cada día. English meaning: Ana opens her notebook. She listens to a word, marks the stressed syllable, and repeats it. She is not seeking perfection; she seeks one clear improvement each day.

Read again for evidence. Identify who acts, what changes, when it happens, and which words establish the relationship between events.
