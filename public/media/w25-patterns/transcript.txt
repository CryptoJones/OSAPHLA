Form and meaning. Simple future; irregular stems; future of probability; time clauses referring to future events.

Model set. La población urbana aumentará. Seguiremos midiendo los resultados. ¿Dónde estará Marta ahora? Cuando termine el estudio, publicaremos los datos. Dentro de cinco años habrá nuevas opciones. English meaning: The urban population will increase. We will continue measuring the results. Where could Marta be now? When the study ends, we will publish the data. In five years there will be new options.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: make forecasts; state commitments; express probability about the present. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
