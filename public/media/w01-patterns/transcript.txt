Form and meaning. Spanish sound–spelling correspondence, the five stable vowels, syllables, stress, written accents, and punctuation.

Model set. Mi nombre se escribe con cinco letras. La h no tiene sonido. Escucho y repito cada palabra. ¿Cuál es la sílaba tónica? La práctica breve y frecuente funciona mejor. English meaning: My name is spelled with five letters. The letter h has no sound. I listen to and repeat every word. Which is the stressed syllable? Brief, frequent practice works better.

Contrastive practice. Change the person, time, number, or level of formality in each model. Preserve the communicative purpose while the grammar changes.

Production check. Say one original sentence for each function: spell a name; recognize a stressed syllable; build a sustainable study loop. Then write it and compare the spoken and written forms.
