Operational objective. By the end of this section you can describe people and relationships, express possession, identify a person precisely. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Adjective agreement and position; possessive adjectives and pronouns; tener; personal a.

Sound and delivery. Diphthongs and smooth linking across word boundaries.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.

Adjectives agree. A variable adjective matches the noun in gender and number: un amigo alto, una amiga alta, unos amigos altos, unas amigas altas. The noun controls the form of the adjective.

Not every adjective changes for gender. Adjectives ending in -e or many consonants commonly keep one singular form: un amigo amable and una amiga amable. They still change for plural number: amigos amables, amigas amables.

Position affects emphasis. Descriptive adjectives usually follow the noun. Some common adjectives can appear before it, sometimes with a change in emphasis or form. First secure agreement; then learn position as part of each expression.
