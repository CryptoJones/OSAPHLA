Operational objective. By the end of this section you can make forecasts, state commitments, express probability about the present. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Simple future; irregular stems; future of probability; time clauses referring to future events.

Sound and delivery. Future endings and sentence stress that distinguishes certainty from speculation.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
