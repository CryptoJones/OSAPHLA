Operational objective. By the end of this section you can state origin and profession, exchange contact numbers, tell dates and times. The focus is useful performance, not isolated terminology.

Language system. Ser for identity and origin; cardinal numbers; dates; telling time with es la and son las.

Sound and delivery. Tap r between vowels; distinguish pero from perro without exaggeration.

Core principle. Notice meaning first, then form. Retrieve the new language aloud, compare it with the model, and repair one feature at a time.
